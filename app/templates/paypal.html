PayPal POS

Export physical items

Each physical item barcode is exported as its own PayPal POS row. Identical items keep the same product name and price, but each barcode has stock 1 or 0.

This lets Rae's Stock keep one parent product while PayPal can scan every individual labelled item.

Import sales / stock back

Upload the PayPal stock CSV after sales. Rae's Stock matches each row by Barcode or SKU.

In Stock = 0 records a sale for that exact physical item. In Stock > 0 restores it Available.

CSV bridge
